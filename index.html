Inside

Take it apart as you scroll.

  1. Printed frame 01 / 04

    Front piece with the click pads, a ribbed palm frame, and the battery tray. White PETG, no supports.

  2. Switches and wheel 02 / 04

    The G305's D2FC-F-7N boards sit on printed mounts under each pad, with the stock wheel and encoder between them.

  3. AAA power 03 / 04

    One AAA lithium cell rides lengthwise in the tray, wired straight to the board.

  4. G305 main board 04 / 04

    The full-length stock board with the HERO sensor and Lightspeed radio, visible through the frame.
